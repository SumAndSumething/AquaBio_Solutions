01 — Who we areApplied biosciences

Formulations,
decoded and rebuilt.

AquaBio Solutions LLP is a Karnataka-headquartered applied-biosciences company. We study, deconstruct, and rebuild bio-formulations—including probiotics, bio-enzymes, biostimulants, fermentation cultures, and marine-algal extracts.

Every reference product becomes a case study: profiled, reformulated around Indian raw materials and cost structures, then manufactured in-house or with contract-production partners.
