AquaBio Solutions LLP · Karnataka, India

Formulations,
decoded and rebuilt.

AquaBio Solutions LLP is a Karnataka-headquartered applied-biosciences company. We study, deconstruct, and rebuild bio-formulations—including probiotics, bio-enzymes, biostimulants, fermentation cultures, and marine-algal extracts.

Every reference product becomes a case study: profiled, reformulated around Indian raw materials and cost structures, then manufactured in-house or with contract-production partners.

04 — How we work · From benchmark to manufacture

Decode.
Reformulate.
Make it real.

Reverse engineering is the method behind the work: profile the reference, understand the composition, and develop a practical alternative for local production.

01

Benchmark & profile

Study a reference product, its actives, strains, ratios, or performance targets.

02

Decode & reformulate

Translate the composition into a reproducible brief using locally available inputs.

03

Trial & manufacture

Move from pilot and validation toward in-house, private-label, or contract production.
