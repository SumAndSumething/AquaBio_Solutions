Who we are

Formulations,
decoded and rebuilt.

AquaBio Solutions LLP is a Karnataka-headquartered applied-biosciences company. We study, deconstruct and rebuild formulations—including probiotics, bio-enzymes, biostimulants, fermentation cultures and marine-algal extracts.

We work across agriculture, aquaculture, horticulture, animal nutrition, wastewater, specialty coffee, personal care and wellness. Each brief is shaped around its application and local context.

The company profile describes a research-led path from reference-product profiling through reformulation and manufacturing, in-house or with production partners.

Aquaculture focus

Healthier ponds
start with balance.

AquaBio's earlier aquaculture concept explores a biological approach to pond conditions: looking at feed waste and fish excreta alongside external-parasite pressure, including Argulus and Lernaea.

The 2021 company post describes research into beneficial bacterial growth as an alternative to harmful chemical treatment. The post frames this as a product concept intended to help reduce parasite recurrence; efficacy and current commercial availability are not independently verified.
